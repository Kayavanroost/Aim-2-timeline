Aim 2 Reanalysis Workplan

A realistic 12-week plan for revising the analysis at approximately five hours per week, with the weighting strategy, analytic sample, confounder measurement, pregnancy-detection assumptions, and manuscript outputs rebuilt in a deliberate order.

Revision priorities

IPTW for confounding

Replace regression adjustment as the primary confounding-control strategy with inverse probability of treatment weighting.

Selection weighting

Use inverse probability weighting to address selection into / remaining in the analytic sample over time, not only confounding.

Reconsider restrictions

Do not restrict to non-immigrants / US-raised participants, and systematically reassess whether other exclusions are necessary.

Pregnancy detection sensitivity

Vary assumptions about time to pregnancy detection, including sensitivity analyses by birth cohort and sex.

Continuous confounders

Use continuous measures where appropriate, including years of parental education rather than broad education categories.

Parental occupation

Add parental occupation as a potential socioeconomic confounder and determine an appropriate harmonized coding strategy.

Analysis ruleFinalize the revised estimands, eligibility criteria, confounder definitions, and weighting strategy before rebuilding the person-month datasets. This should reduce repeated re-coding later.

12-week timeline

Week 1
Plan

Lock down the revised analysis plan

  • Write a 1–2 page analysis specification.
  • Define the estimand for each time-to-event analysis.
  • Specify exposure, outcome, censoring, confounding, and selection processes.
  • List every current sample restriction and decide which need to remain.
  • Pre-specify IPTW, selection-weighting, stabilization, and truncation approach.
5 hrs
Week 2
Cohort

Rebuild the analytic sample

  • Remove the non-immigrant / US-raised restriction.
  • Reconsider all other exclusions one by one.
  • Generate a sample-flow table with N after each exclusion.
  • Compare old versus revised sample characteristics and event counts.
5 hrs
Week 3
Variables

Rebuild confounders

  • Construct parental education in years.
  • Identify and harmonize parental occupation measures.
  • Check distributions, missingness, and implausible values.
  • Update the confounder list / DAG specification.
5 hrs
Week 4
Weights

Build selection / attrition weights

  • Define the selection process and relevant time points.
  • Fit probability-of-selection / remaining-observed models.
  • Generate stabilized inverse probability of selection weights.
  • Inspect weight distributions and extreme values.
5 hrs
Week 5
Weights

Build confounding IPTWs

  • Fit exposure models separately for the two causal questions.
  • Generate stabilized IPTWs.
  • Combine confounding and selection weights as appropriate.
  • Implement pre-specified truncation strategy.
5 hrs
Week 6
Diagnostics

Evaluate the weights

  • Assess positivity and exposure overlap.
  • Calculate standardized mean differences before and after weighting.
  • Calculate effective sample size.
  • Revise propensity models if balance is inadequate.
  • Save balance plots / diagnostics for the supplement.
5 hrs
Week 7
Primary analysis

Run weighted survival analyses

  • Estimate pregnancy detection → child marriage association using the weighted model.
  • Estimate child marriage → teen childbirth association using the weighted model.
  • Check relevant survival-model assumptions.
  • Compare new estimates with original crude and regression-adjusted results.
5 hrs
Week 8
Sensitivity

Pregnancy-detection sensitivity analyses

  • Pre-specify plausible alternative time-to-detection assumptions.
  • Rerun reproductive trajectory analyses under alternative assumptions.
  • Rerun pregnancy detection → marriage analyses.
  • Vary detection assumptions by birth cohort.
  • Vary detection assumptions by sex.
5 hrs
Week 9
Robustness

Run additional robustness analyses

  • Compare reasonable weight-truncation thresholds.
  • Compare confounding-only IPTW with confounding + selection weighting.
  • Retain regression-adjusted estimates as a secondary comparison.
  • Investigate materially different or unexpected estimates.
5 hrs
Week 10
Outputs

Regenerate tables and figures

  • Update sample characteristics table.
  • Update reproductive trajectory figures.
  • Replace adjusted-model tables with weighted effect estimates.
  • Add balance / weight diagnostics and sensitivity-analysis outputs.
  • Audit all analytic Ns across outputs.
5 hrs
Week 11
Writing

Rewrite Methods and Results

  • Rewrite sample and eligibility criteria.
  • Rewrite confounder measurement.
  • Document IPTW and selection-weight construction and diagnostics.
  • Rewrite primary and sensitivity-analysis results.
  • Update all table and figure references.
5 hrs
Week 12
Finalize

Discussion and final quality control

  • Revise discussion and interpretation based on weighted estimates.
  • Update limitations on residual confounding, selection, and pregnancy-detection assumptions.
  • Update abstract.
  • Audit every number across abstract, text, tables, and figures.
  • Clean and annotate final reproducible analysis code.
5 hrs

Key final outputs

  • Revised analytic cohort and sample-flow documentation
  • Confounding IPTWs and inverse probability of selection weights
  • Weight distribution, overlap, balance, and effective-sample-size diagnostics
  • Primary weighted survival estimates for both causal questions
  • Pregnancy-detection sensitivity analyses by cohort and sex
  • Comparison of crude, original regression-adjusted, IPTW, and IPTW + selection-weighted estimates
  • Updated tables, figures, supplement, Methods, Results, Discussion, and abstract
